**Charity Visit & Community Service** was a social outreach activity organized by our school community on February 25, 2025, at an orphanage in Jayapura to deliver material support and build warm social connections. Before the visit, I participated in logistical planning, including organizing donation packages and coordinating transportation needs, followed by direct interactive games and shared sessions with the children during the event. This activity ran warmly and deepened my sense of social empathy, collaborative teamwork, and interpersonal communication within the community.

<div class="lang-id hidden">

**Kunjungan Kasih & Pengabdian Masyarakat** merupakan kegiatan bakti sosial komunitas sekolah yang dilaksanakan pada 25 Februari 2025 di sebuah panti asuhan di Jayapura untuk menyalurkan bantuan kebutuhan serta mempererat kepedulian sosial. Sebelum hari pelaksanaan, saya terlibat langsung dalam perencanaan logistik seperti pendataan paket bantuan dan pengaturan transportasi, kemudian mendampingi anak-anak panti asuhan melalui permainan edukatif dan sesi berbagi cerita saat acara berlangsung. Kegiatan ini berjalan dengan hangat dan menumbuhkan empati sosial, kekompakan kerja tim, serta kemampuan komunikasi interpersonal saya.
