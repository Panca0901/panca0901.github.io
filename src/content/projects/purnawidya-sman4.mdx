**SMAN 4 Jayapura Graduation Committee** was a large-scale school event management project held on May 5, 2025, in Jayapura to organize a formal, orderly, and memorable graduation ceremony for the class of 2025. Serving on the organizing committee, I took an active role in cross-division coordination to synchronize technical workflows, stage transitions, and logistical preparations across multiple teams. Thanks to solid teamwork and continuous communication among student officers, teachers, and graduating students, the ceremony ran smoothly and right on schedule, strengthening my leadership and real-time event management skills.

**Kepanitiaan Purnawidya SMAN 4 Jayapura** merupakan kegiatan manajemen acara sekolah skala besar yang dilaksanakan pada 5 Mei 2025 di Jayapura untuk menyelenggarakan upacara kelulusan yang khidmat, tertib, dan berkesan bagi siswa angkatan 2025. Dalam struktur kepanitiaan ini, saya berperan aktif melakukan koordinasi lintas divisi untuk menyelaraskan alur teknis acara, transisi susunan kegiatan, serta kesiapan logistik di lapangan. Berkat kerja sama tim yang solid dan komunikasi intensif antara panitia, guru, dan peserta wisuda, seluruh rangkaian acara terlaksana dengan lancar dan tepat waktu.
