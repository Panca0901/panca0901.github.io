**OSIS × Ganesha Operation Strategic Collaboration** was a partnership planning meeting held on July 16, 2025, in Jayapura between student council representatives and the Ganesha Operation educational team. During this session, I actively contributed to discussing joint academic support initiatives, university entrance preparation programs, and leadership development activities for high school students. This experience sharpened my external communication, professional negotiation, and strategic program-planning skills in building mutually beneficial educational partnerships.

**Kolaborasi Strategis OSIS × Ganesha Operation** merupakan pertemuan perencanaan kemitraan yang dilaksanakan pada 16 Juli 2025 di Jayapura antara perwakilan pengurus OSIS dan tim lembaga pendidikan Ganesha Operation. Dalam forum ini, saya berperan aktif membahas rancangan program dukungan akademik, persiapan masuk perguruan tinggi, serta kegiatan pengembangan kepemimpinan bagi para siswa. Pengalaman ini mengasah kemampuan komunikasi eksternal, negosiasi profesional, serta perencanaan program strategis dalam membangun kerja sama pendidikan yang bermanfaat.
