**Mathematics Bootcamp Training Program** is an intensive academic preparation and skill-building program held in Jayapura from August to October 2026, designed to strengthen mathematical logic, foundational statistics, and technical data-processing capabilities for undergraduate studies in Statistics. Throughout the bootcamp, I engaged in rigorous quantitative problem-solving exercises, learned how to optimize spreadsheet functions and data management workflows using MS Office, and practiced structuring scientific papers according to academic writing standards. Beyond technical mastery, the program also emphasized personal development, analytical discipline, and structured thinking when approaching complex numerical problems. As a result of this training, I significantly improved my precision in data calculation, gained confidence in interpreting statistical concepts, and built a strong academic readiness that directly supports my coursework and laboratory reports at university.
